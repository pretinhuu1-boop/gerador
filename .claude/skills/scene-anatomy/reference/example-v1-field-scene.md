# Example V1 — Field scene (Sarah + Robert, golden hour)

The contemplative-pacing reference: **16 shots in 45 seconds** (~2.8 s/shot).
Recovered verbatim from a canonical "SCENE ANATOMY" reel via frame OCR. Use
this as the gold-standard for emotional / breakup / two-character outdoor
scenes.

## Logline

After a long buildup, Sarah confronts Robert in a wide dry-grass field at
golden hour about a lie spanning months. The scene moves through
confrontation → emotional break → reconciliation, ending with a question
about how much time they have left.

## Global anchors

- **Characters**: Sarah (40s, shoulder-length hair, navy peacoat, gold pendant
  at collarbone), Robert (50s, salt-and-pepper beard, olive jacket).
- **Location**: open dry-weed field, low golden sun, cold wind.
- **Tonal anchor**: contemporary indie drama, 35mm, shallow depth of field.
- **Three-act split**: shots 1–5 setup → 6–13 conflict → 14–16 reconciliation.

## Full script

```
SHOT 1
  [CAM] WS, TRACKING BACKWARD
  [ACT] Robert glances down at his own hand, then looks back at Sarah.
        Wind blows hair across Sarah's cheek; she ignores it
  [ENV] Fading amber light on the dry weeds, constant wind hum.

SHOT 2
  [CAM] MCU, LOCKOFF, eye level on Sarah's profile from her left
  [ACT] Sarah keeps her gaze locked forward, her chin lifting slightly
        as she speaks
  [AUDIO] Sarah: "When did you find out."
  [ENV] Wind rustling.

SHOT 3
  [CAM] MCU, HANDHELD, OTS from behind Robert's left shoulder
  [ACT] Robert reaches his right hand across to touch Sarah's left arm
        sleeve. Sarah instantly pulls her arm away without breaking stride,
        opening a physical gap. Robert's hand hangs in the empty air
  [AUDIO] Robert: "Sarah, can we just—"
          Sarah: "I asked you when."

SHOT 4
  [CAM] MCU, LOCKOFF, slight low angle, three-quarter view of Robert
  [ACT] Robert drops his hand back to his side, letting out a long, visible
        exhale through his nose. His eyes shift to the right, jaw tightening
  [AUDIO] Robert: "...February. The week before Venice."

SHOT 5
  [CAM] WS, STEADICAM SIDE-TRACKING
  [ACT] Sarah now ahead of Robert. Her arms unfold and her right hand
        presses flat against the gold pendant at her collarbone. Her stride
        slows down, her weight hesitating on her next step
  [AUDIO] Footsteps crunching slower on dry soil

SHOT 6
  [CAM] MCU, eye level on Sarah
  [ACT] Sarah abruptly plants her left foot and pivots her body right to
        face Robert head-on, eyes locking onto him. Robert steps into the
        right edge of the frame, pulling up inches from her face
  [AUDIO] Sarah: "(sigh)... You held my hand, on every bridge in that city."

SHOT 7
  [CAM] MS, HANDHELD, profile view, Robert Frame-Right, Sarah Frame-Left
  [ACT] Sarah's arms hang at her sides, her right hand making a sharp
        flicking gesture as she speaks. Robert steps half a pace forward,
        leaning in desperately
  [AUDIO] Sarah: "You laughed at that waiter who spilled the wine."
          Robert: "Because I meant all of it—"

SHOT 8
  [CAM] MCU, eye level, on Sarah
  [ACT] Sarah's eyes fill with tears, her lower lip trembling as her voice
        drops to a raw, quiet tone
  [AUDIO] Sarah: "Every day... EVERY DAY! you didn't tell me was a day you
          let me live in a world that doesn't exist."
  [ENV] Cold howling wind.

SHOT 9
  [CAM] MCU, OTS HANDHELD, over Sarah's left shoulder looking at Robert
  [ACT] Robert's mouth parts, jaw working silently as he searches her
        eyes. Sarah slowly drops her chin, breaking eye contact and
        turning away
  [AUDIO] Breath catching, ambient wind.

SHOT 10
  [CAM] WS, perpendicular to their path
  [ACT] Robert remains frozen center-frame. Sarah walks past him, she
        continues walking right-to-left, increasing the physical distance
  [AUDIO] Wind rustling, footsteps fading

SHOT 11
  [CAM] MCU, on Robert
  [ACT] Robert halts, his brows furrowing as his lower lip catches under
        his teeth. His chin lifts as he projects his voice toward Sarah
        off-screen
  [AUDIO] Robert: "...Sarah!"

SHOT 12
  [CAM] WS, HANDHELD, low angle through foreground dried stalks
  [ACT] Sarah stops mid-stride, facing camera with her back to him. Robert
        takes a single step forward, his hands opening slightly at his
        sides, fingers spread. Sarah's shoulders rise with a slow inhale,
        staying still
  [AUDIO] Robert: "I'm... telling you now. I'm telling you here..."
  [ENV] Dry grass rustling.

SHOT 13
  [CAM] CU, LOCKOFF, dead-on framing of Robert
  [ACT] Robert takes a deep breath, his chest rising under his shirt. He
        looks up at the sky, scans the field, his eyes glossy and wet with
        unshed tears, before looking back down at Sarah
  [AUDIO] Robert: "...because this is the only place I've ever known how
          to be afraid."

SHOT 14
  [CAM] MS, HANDHELD, OTS over Robert's right shoulder
  [ACT] Sarah's shoulder drops. She slowly pivots her head and hips,
        turning to face him. Her expression is soft, eyes wet, anger
        completely gone
  [ENV] Wind blowing hair across her cheek.

SHOT 15
  [CAM] TCU, 85mm telephoto two-shot compressing the gap
  [ACT] Robert's eyes search her face. Sarah's expression softening. She
        gives a micro-nod, her lips parting
  [AUDIO] Sarah: "How long do we have."
  [ACT] Robert slowly closes and opens his eyes, a tremor on his lower lip.

SHOT 16
  [CAM] WS, SLOW DOLLY-OUT
  [ACT] They stand four steps apart, staring at each other. The camera
        slowly pulls back, making them smaller as the vast field and cold
        sky dominate the frame
  [AUDIO] Wind through dry grass, a single distant bird call
```

## Why this script works

- **Movement rhythm**: WS (1) → MCU (2-4) → WS (5) → MCU (6-9) → WS (10) →
  MCU/CU (11-15) → WS (16). The pattern is *wide-tight-tight-tight-wide-...*
  — every 4–5 shots returns to wide for breathing room.
- **Audio escalation**: ambient (1) → first dialogue (2) → interruption (3)
  → reveal (4) → silence with footsteps (5) → escalating monologue (6-8) →
  silence with breath (9-10) → break-through monologue (11-13) → quiet
  reconciliation (14-15) → ambient resolution (16).
- **One [TRANS]-worthy beat without explicit [TRANS]**: shot 9→10's
  "breaking eye contact" is the emotional pivot but stays inside [ACT].
- **Match Shot opportunity not taken**: shots 4 and 11 share framing
  conceptually but are deliberately NOT matched — to underscore that the
  emotional landscape has shifted.
