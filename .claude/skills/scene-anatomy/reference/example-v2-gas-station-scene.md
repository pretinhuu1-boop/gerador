# Example V2 — Gas station scene (Mark + Robert, neon night)

The kinetic-pacing + multi-location reference: **21 shots in 45 seconds**
(~2.1 s/shot). Recovered verbatim from a canonical "SCENE ANATOMY" reel via
frame OCR. Use this as the gold-standard for surreal / comedic / movement-
heavy scenes that span multiple sub-locations within one continuous take.

## Logline

After leaving a convenience store at night, Mark recounts a surreal dream to
a deadpan Robert. The conversation moves from store-front → walking →
across the car roof → inside the car → outside the car → driving away.
Tonal beats oscillate between Mark's surreal description and Robert's
clipped denials.

## Global anchors

- **Characters**: Robert (50s, weathered, salt-and-pepper, plaid jacket +
  navy puffer vest), Mark (30s, yellow rain windbreaker, dark sweater).
- **Locations** (in order): gas station storefront → wet asphalt walking →
  teal sedan → car interior → car exterior passenger side → car driving out.
- **Tonal anchor**: neo-noir comedic, neon-lit, wet asphalt reflections, 35mm.
- **Recurring [ENV] motif**: "humming red neon canopy", neon hum, wet
  asphalt squeaks.
- **Three-act split**: shots 1–7 walking conversation → 8–14 across car
  roof confrontation → 15–21 interior / departure.

## Full script

```
SHOT 1
  [CAM] WS, STATIC
  [ACT] Robert exits store first with bag in right hand; Mark follows in
        yellow windbreaker, hands in pockets. They walk forward across
        damp, red-lit asphalt
  [AUDIO] Door chime, footsteps, neon hum

SHOT 2
  [CAM] MS, PROFILE TRACKING L-TO-R. Robert frame-left, Mark frame-right
  [ACT] Robert looks straight ahead. Mark turns his head to him, pulling
        his left hand out to point upward
  [AUDIO] Mark: "I had the weirdest dream last night."

SHOT 3
  [CAM] CU, TRACKING, 3/4 FRONT ON ROBERT
  [ACT] Robert keeps staring dead ahead, face flat; his eyes flick once
        left toward Mark, then snap back forward without breaking stride
  [AUDIO] Bag crinkles. Mark (O.S.): "You were in it."

SHOT 4
  [CAM] MCU, HANDHELD TRACKING ON MARK
  [ACT] Mark gestures with both hands, painting ceiling and shelves in the
        air. He looks right at Robert, then ahead, then back to Robert
  [AUDIO] Mark: "We were in a grocery store but everything was upside down.
          Like — the shelves were on the ceiling."

SHOT 5
  [CAM] WS, STATIC FROM BEHIND. Robert frame-left, Mark frame-right
  [ACT] They walk away from camera. Robert's posture is rigid. Mark walks
        a half-step behind, leaning in toward him
  [AUDIO] Echoing steps. Mark (O.S.): "And you were just..."

SHOT 6
  [CAM] CU, TRACKING BACKWARDS ON MARK
  [ACT] Mark's hands drop to his sides. He smiles softly looking slightly
        off-camera right toward Robert. His pace slows slightly
  [AUDIO] Mark: "...shopping. Like it was normal."

SHOT 7
  [CAM] MS, PROFILE TRACKING L-TO-R. Robert frame-left, Mark frame-right
  [ACT] Match Shot 2. Robert walks unmoved, bag in right hand. Mark walks
        beside him, hands back in yellow windbreaker pockets, staring ahead
  [AUDIO] Footsteps fade into quiet night wind

SHOT 8
  [CAM] WS, STATIC
  [ACT] They reach the sedan. Robert opens the driver's door, tosses the
        plastic bag onto the seat, and shuts it. Mark stands by the
        passenger door, restlessly shifting his weight from foot to foot,
        tapping his thigh
  [AUDIO] Car door opens and shuts, keys jingle.
          Mark: "And then you turned around..."

SHOT 9
  [CAM] MCU, SLIGHT SWAY ON MARK
  [ACT] Mark rolls his shoulders lazily. His hands cup together in front
        of his chest, miming holding something delicate. He glances
        off-camera left
  [AUDIO] Mark: "...and you were holding a fish. Not a dead fish."

SHOT 10
  [CAM] CU, CLOSE-UP ON ROBERT
  [ACT] Robert leans his forearm flat on the car roof, staring directly
        across at Mark. His face is blank; his eyes blink once slowly. His
        jaw clenches slightly
  [AUDIO] Mark (O.S.): "A living fish."

SHOT 11
  [CAM] MS, SLIGHT ARC ON MARK
  [ACT] Mark steps back. His feet shuffle in a slow nervous dance. His
        right hand gestures outward, then his left hand cups his ear, head
        tilted
  [AUDIO] Shoe scuffs. Mark: "And you said something to me but I couldn't
          hear it because the fish was — I think the fish was humming?"

SHOT 12
  [CAM] MS, STATIC TWO-SHOT ACROSS CAR ROOF
  [ACT] Robert is frame-left leaning static on the roof. Mark is
        frame-right, shifting. Robert turns his head to look directly at Mark
  [AUDIO] Robert: "I don't dream."

SHOT 13
  [CAM] MCU, HANDHELD ON MARK
  [ACT] Mark's shuffling freezes; his posture squares offended. Then his
        weight shifts again, and his right hand makes a slight dismissive
        upward flick
  [AUDIO] Mark: "Everyone dreams, Robert."

SHOT 14
  [CAM] CU, STATIC ON ROBERT
  [ACT] Red neon hits Robert's weathered face. He holds Mark's gaze for a
        silent beat, expression completely stony
  [AUDIO] Robert: "Not about fish."
  [TRANS] Robert turns away, breaking eye contact.

SHOT 15
  [CAM] MCU, HANDHELD (interior, driver side)
  [ACT] Robert slides into the driver's seat, pulling the door shut.
        Through the glass, Mark is seen outside, beginning a silent
        rhythmic shoulder-roll and weight-shifting dance
  [AUDIO] Car door thuds shut, muffled breathing, faint rain

SHOT 16
  [CAM] WS, LOW ANGLE STATIC
  [ACT] The car sits in the wet lot. Inside, Robert is a still silhouette.
        Outside, Mark continues his silent "dance," eyes closed as he
        slowly shuffles and glides around the front hood of the car to the
        passenger side
  [AUDIO] Squeak of shoes on wet asphalt

SHOT 17
  [CAM] INT. PASSENGER SEAT LOOKING AT ROBERT
  [ACT] Dashboard glow lights Robert. He stares forward through the
        windshield, his fingers tapping the steering wheel once. His eyes
        drift right toward the passenger window
  [AUDIO] Rhythmic tapping on the steering wheel

SHOT 18
  [CAM] POV ROBERT THROUGH PASSENGER WINDOW
  [ACT] Through the glass, Mark is on the passenger side executing a
        clumsy, silent spin, yellow raincoat catching the neon light as he
        mouths words to himself
  [AUDIO] Muffled squeaking, neon hum

SHOT 19
  [CAM] INT. CU ON ROBERT
  [ACT] Robert watches Mark through the window. His stern expression
        softens slightly. He leans his head toward the passenger glass
  [AUDIO] Robert: "You coming?"

SHOT 20
  [CAM] MS ON MARK, EXT. PASSENGER SIDE
  [ACT] Mark stops shuffling, plants his feet, and looks at Robert's
        silhouette behind the glass. A wide, slow grin takes over his
        face. He quickly grabs the door handle, opens it, and hops inside
  [AUDIO] Door handle clicks, door swings open

SHOT 21
  [CAM] WS, STATIC
  [ACT] The teal sedan's headlights sweep across the wet asphalt as it
        pulls out of the frame. The empty parking lot remains, reflecting
        the humming red neon canopy
  [AUDIO] Engine roars, tires splash on puddles, fading to quiet wind
```

## Why this script works

- **Location chain**: 1-storefront → 2-7-walking → 8-14-across car roof →
  15-16-interior/exterior transition → 17-19-interior POV → 20-21-departure.
  Each location gets at least 2 shots, no location gets more than 7.
- **Match Shot 2**: shot 7 explicitly matches shot 2's framing — this
  cements the audience's spatial sense after the L-to-R tracking detour
  through shots 3–6.
- **Explicit [TRANS] at shot 14→15**: the door close is the act break.
  Marking it as `[TRANS]` ensures Seedance gives the cut a deliberate
  rhythm rather than just snapping.
- **POV reversal (shots 17–19)**: switching to inside-car POV in act 3
  changes the audience's relationship from observer to driver. This is the
  payoff that makes shot 19's softening land.
- **Closer is sound-only**: shot 21's [AUDIO] does all the work; no
  dialogue. Mirrors V1's closing shot — both scripts use ambient-only
  closers to release tension.

## Continuity tips lifted from this scene

- **Yellow raincoat** is mentioned in shots 1, 5, 18, 20 — a costume
  callback functioning as a visual leitmotif.
- **Red neon** mentioned in shots 1, 14, 16, 18, 21 — anchors the night
  palette.
- **Bag in right hand** persists through shots 1, 7, 8 — a continuity prop
  that helps Seedance keep the character consistent across cuts.
